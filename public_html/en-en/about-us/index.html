About us

A small agency
with a long view.

Zyra exists to help small and medium businesses modernise without getting lost in jargon or oversized promises.

Who we are

Technology with its feet on the ground.

We're a technology agency focused on web development, automation and artificial intelligence. We build solutions meant to solve real business problems, without unnecessary complexity or promises we can't keep.

We started building professional websites, but the vision goes further: custom software, automations and AI agents trained on each business's real information. We don't promise magic or inflated numbers: we'd rather be a new agency that's honest about where it is.

Mission
Helping small and medium businesses solve real problems with technology, without selling them more than they need.
Vision
Becoming the go-to agency for businesses that want to go digital without losing control of how and why.
How we work
With clear contracts, direct communication, and no selling features we couldn't maintain later.
