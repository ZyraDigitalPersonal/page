Cookie policy

What gets stored in your browser when you visit the ZYRA website.

What they are and how we use them

Cookies and similar technologies (such as the browser's local storage) store small pieces of data on your device. This website uses two kinds: technical ones, which the site needs to remember your preferences and which do not require permission, and marketing ones, which are only activated if you accept them in the cookie notice. We do not use analytics tools today.

Technical cookies (always on)

NameTypePurposeDuration
zyra_langFirst-party cookieRemember the language you choose so you land on that version when you visit digitalzyra.com1 year
zyra-langLocal storageRemember the language in the browserUntil you delete it
zyra-themeLocal storageRemember the light or dark themeUntil you delete it
zyra-consentLocal storageStore your cookie decision6 months

Marketing cookies (only if you accept them)

If you enable the marketing category, Google services are loaded and set their own cookies:

ServicePurposeExample cookiesDuration
Google Ads (gtag.js tag)Measure whether our ad campaigns bring enquiries_gcl_au on digitalzyra.com; Google cookies on google.com and doubleclick.netUp to 90 days for the first-party one; per Google for third-party ones
Google AdSenseShow ads on this website, personalised or not depending on your choiceGoogle cookies on doubleclick.net and google.com (for example IDE, __gads, __gpi)Up to 13 months, per Google

Google explains which cookies it uses and why on its page about how Google uses cookies. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's Ads Settings.

Sign in with Google for reviews

If you choose to post a review by signing in with Google, Google's identity service is loaded and may use its own cookies for that sign-in. This only happens if you press that button.

How long your decision lasts

We keep your choice for six months. After that, or if we change the categories, we ask again. The Spanish Data Protection Agency considers it good practice not to keep the decision for more than 24 months.

Changing your mind

You can review your preferences at any time with the button below or the Cookie preferences link in the footer. You can also delete cookies and local storage from your browser settings: the site will keep working, but it will not remember your language or theme.
