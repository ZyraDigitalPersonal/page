How long we keep it

  • Requests that do not lead to a contract: up to 12 months after the last contact, in case you pick up the conversation again. Then they are deleted.
  • Clients: for as long as the relationship lasts and then for the periods required by law for contractual and tax obligations.
  • Reviews: while they are published or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Cookies: the period stated in the cookie policy.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of your data, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to zyradigitalpersonal@gmail.com. We will reply within one month at most. If you think we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

Security and minors

The website only works over an encrypted connection (https), forms are protected against automated submissions and access to requests is limited to the team. The services on this website are not aimed at children under 14.
