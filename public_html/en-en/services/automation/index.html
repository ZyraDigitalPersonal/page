Copying data from a form into a sheet, sending the same reminder every afternoon, downloading invoices from email and filing them in a folder. They are small tasks, but they repeat every day and each one is a chance to make a mistake. Automating them means connecting the tools you already use so those steps happen on their own and a person only steps in when judgement is needed.

The problem it solves

A task is a good candidate when it repeats often, always follows the same steps, its data is already digital and a mistake costs money or customers. If one of those conditions is missing, the usual approach is to automate only the mechanical part and leave the decision to a person. We explain it in more detail, with a calculator, in the guide Which tasks in your business are worth automating (in Spanish).

Make, Zapier or n8n: how we choose

We choose the tool for the case, not the other way round:

  • Zapier: the easiest to maintain, with the most ready-made integrations. Fits short flows and cases where you want to be able to edit them yourself.
  • Make: more flexible for flows with several branches, filters and data transformations.
  • n8n: its source code is public (a “fair-code” licence) and it can be installed on your own server, which gives more control over where data goes. It needs a bit more technical maintenance.
  • Direct API connections, email, Slack or WhatsApp when there is no ready-made integration or a small custom development makes more sense.

These platforms charge by usage. Accounts are created in your name and the quote states which plan you need.

Scope: what is in and what is out

Included: mapping the tasks that repeat, connecting your tools, flows that alert you when something fails, testing with real data before switching on, documentation and initial adjustments. The usual timeline is 1 to 3 weeks.

Not included: switching tools unless necessary (it is the last option) or automating decisions that need judgement, such as answering a complaint or approving a payment.

What we need from you

  • The exact steps of the task, including odd cases: what happens if data is missing or the customer has no WhatsApp.
  • Access to the tools involved, with a user that has only the permissions needed.
  • Real examples to test with: forms, emails or orders from recent weeks.
  • Who receives the alerts when a flow fails.

How it is measured

Before starting we note how much time goes into the task and how many errors happen. For one or two weeks the automation runs alongside the manual process so results can be compared. Afterwards the same things are measured: hours recovered, errors avoided and failure alerts received.

Limits worth knowing

  • Every automation fails at some point: a tool changes, a password expires or data arrives in an unexpected format. That is why flows send alerts and are documented.
  • Automating a confusing process only produces errors faster. If the process is not clear, we sort it out first.
  • If the automation moves customer data, that data passes through the connected tools. Their terms need checking and it should be mentioned in your privacy policy.

FAQ

Common questions about this service.

Do I have to change tools?

No. We automate on top of what you already use whenever it can be connected; changing tools is the last option.

What happens if a flow fails?

Flows send an alert when something breaks and are documented so they can be reviewed without depending on us.

Zapier, Make or n8n?

It depends on the flow and on who will maintain it. Zapier is the simplest, Make is more flexible for complex logic and n8n can be hosted on your own server. We recommend one and explain why.

Are the tools' subscription fees included?

No. The platforms charge by usage and the accounts are in your name. The quote states which plan you need for the expected volume.

Which tasks are not worth automating?

Those that change every month, those done a few times a year and sensitive conversations such as complaints or overdue payments. For those a draft can be prepared, but a person should send it.
