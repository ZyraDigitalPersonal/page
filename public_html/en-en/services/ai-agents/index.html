FAQ

Common questions about this service.

Can the agent make things up?

It is trained on your documentation and given limits: what it doesn't know goes to a person instead of being improvised.

Can it handle bookings?

Yes, when your booking tool allows integration. If not, it collects the details and notifies your team.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the real scope, so we don't publish fixed rates. Tell us what you need over WhatsApp or the form and we send a closed quote, with no open-ended items.

What if the scope changes mid-project?

We talk it through and adjust budget and timing before continuing — extra work is never added without you knowing first.
