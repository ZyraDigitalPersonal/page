If your business gets the same questions every day by WhatsApp, on the website or by phone (opening hours, prices, whether there is a slot on Thursday), part of that work can be handed to an AI assistant. The other part, the one that needs judgement, should still reach a person. Our job is to separate the two properly.

The problem it solves

Repeated questions interrupt work, and those that arrive out of hours or while the team is busy often go unanswered. An AI agent replies straight away using your information, collects the details of whoever writes or calls, and passes the case to the team when it should not handle it alone.

Three kinds of assistant

TypeWhat it doesMain risk
Answering chatbotAnswers questions from the information you give it, on the website or WhatsAppAnswering something that is not in that information
Agent with actionsAlso checks or changes things: the calendar, an order, a CRMTaking the wrong action, such as double-booking
Voice agentAnswers calls: understands what the person says, replies with a synthetic voice and takes a note or transfers the callMishearing because of noise or accent, and the conversation feeling slow

Our usual advice is to start with a well-made answering assistant and add actions or voice once you know what your customers really ask. We explain it in the guide Chatbot or AI agent for customer service (in Spanish).

How it works

  • Knowledge base: the agent answers from your documents (services, prices, policies, FAQs), not from what the model “knows” from the internet.
  • Rules: what it must never say, when to hand over to a person and to whom, and the tone of its replies.
  • Channels: the website, WhatsApp Business or the phone, depending on where your customers write or call.
  • AI model: we use models from OpenAI, Anthropic or others depending on language, cost and the kind of task.
  • Review: conversations are reviewed regularly to correct answers and fill gaps in the information.

Scope: what is in and what is out

Included: defining the use cases with you, preparing the information it works from, integrating it on the website and WhatsApp, setting up hand-over rules and testing it with real questions before publishing. The usual timeline is 2 to 4 weeks.

Not included: automated medical, legal or financial advice. In those areas the agent can inform and hand over, but not decide.

Keep in mind: AI model providers and telephony or messaging providers charge by usage. We explain what that cost depends on before starting.

What we need from you

  • The most frequent questions with their exact answer. If you do not have them written down, they come out of your latest WhatsApp messages and emails.
  • Services, prices and policies up to date: cancellations, deposits, refunds.
  • What it must never say and the cases it must always pass to a person.
  • Access to the channels: WhatsApp Business account, phone number or the website where it will be installed.

How we test it

Before publishing, we run it through a set of real questions with the expected answer next to each one: questions written in different ways and with typos, questions whose answer is not in the information (it must admit it does not know), attempts to get a discount out of it and an angry customer. When it fails, the fix is almost always in the information or the rules, and the whole set is run again.

How to tell whether it works

What share of enquiries it resolves on its own, how many it hands over and why, how many answers had to be corrected in the review and how long customers wait for a reply. If hand-overs do not drop over time, information is missing or there are too many rules.

Limits and obligations

  • An AI model can make mistakes. Rules, testing and review reduce that risk but do not remove it, which is why there is always a way to talk to a person.
  • The EU AI Act requires telling people they are talking to an AI system. The agent says so at the start of the conversation.
  • Conversations contain personal data: you need to decide where they are stored, for how long, and state it in your privacy policy.

FAQ

Common questions about this service.

Can the agent make up answers?

It is trained on your documentation and given limits: what it does not know, it hands over to a person instead of improvising. Even so, conversations are reviewed to catch errors.

Can it take bookings?

Yes, when the booking tool allows integration. If not, it collects the details and alerts your team.

Can it also answer phone calls?

Yes, with a voice agent that answers the phone, handles the usual questions and takes a note or transfers the call to a person. We recommend starting with text if your frequent questions are not yet clear.

Will the customer know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. It is an obligation under the EU AI Act and it also avoids misunderstandings. The agent says so at the start.

What does the cost depend on?

The channels (website, WhatsApp, phone), whether it only answers or also takes actions, the integrations and the volume of conversations, which drives what AI and messaging providers charge.
