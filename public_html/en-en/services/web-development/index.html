Most people looking for a local business do it on their phone, with a specific question: is it open today, how much does it cost, how do I get there, how do I book. A useful website answers those questions in seconds. One that does not becomes a brochure nobody finishes reading.

The problem it solves

We usually find three situations: businesses that only exist on social media or on their Google profile, businesses with an old website that does not work well on mobile, and businesses with a recent website built from a template that does not explain what makes them different. In all three cases the result is the same: customers have to ask on WhatsApp what the website should already tell them.

A website is also the only channel you fully control. Social networks change their rules and their reach; your domain and your content stay yours.

Scope: what is in and what is out

Included: bespoke design (no closed templates), layout adapted to mobile and tablet, contact form, domain and SSL certificate set-up, basic SEO and one round of revisions. By basic SEO we mean that every page has its own title and description, a correct heading structure, a sitemap, and that the site can be crawled and indexed without errors.

Not included: ad campaigns, social media management or ongoing SEO work. Nor do we promise a specific position on Google: nobody can guarantee it, and you should be wary of anyone who does.

What we need from you

  • Information about the business: services, opening hours, the area you cover and the questions customers ask most.
  • Real photos of the premises, the team or the work. Stock photos do not build trust; if you have none, we discuss it before starting.
  • Logo and colours, if you have them.
  • Details for the legal pages: name or company name, tax ID and address of the website owner.
  • Access to the domain and hosting, if they already exist.
  • A contact person who can review and answer questions during the project.

The timeline depends a lot on this: an essential website can be ready in one or two weeks if the copy and photos arrive at the start.

How we check that it works

Before going live we go through the website with the same 25-point checklist (in Spanish) we publish in our guides: the phone number can be tapped, the form arrives, it loads quickly on an ordinary phone (we measure it with PageSpeed Insights) and there are no broken links. After that, the question that matters is a different one: are more calls, messages or bookings coming from the website? Asking every new customer how they found you is enough to know.

Limits worth knowing

  • A website does not bring customers on its own: people need to reach it (Google profile, social media, recommendations) and it needs content that answers what they are looking for.
  • The included round of revisions covers adjustments to what was agreed. New pages or features are quoted separately, before doing them.
  • We prepare the legal pages with your details, but the website owner is responsible for their content. If your activity has special requirements (health, online sales, minors), a professional should review them.

FAQ

Common questions about this service.

Who keeps the domain and hosting?

You do. We can set it up for you or work on the one you already have, but the accounts are always in your name.

Can I ask for changes later?

Yes. One round of revisions is included and, if you want to expand later, it is quoted separately before anything is touched.

Do you use WordPress or build from scratch?

It depends on who will update the site and how often. If you will change content often yourself, a content management system makes sense; if the site changes little, a custom-built site is usually faster and needs less maintenance. We explain the reasoning in the quote.

What does the price depend on?

Mainly the number of pages, the features (bookings, catalogue, private area), whether the copy needs writing, the languages and integrations with other tools. With that information we give you a fixed quote.

Will you help me show up on Google?

We leave the site ready for Google to crawl and index it and register it in Search Console. Ranking high also depends on your Google profile, reviews and content, and that cannot be guaranteed.
