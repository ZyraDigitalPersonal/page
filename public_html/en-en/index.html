FAQ

What people usually ask us.

How long does a typical project take?

An essential website is usually ready in 1–2 weeks. Custom software or automation depend on scope, and we agree a schedule after the first call.

How does payment work?

It depends on the service: fixed projects are usually split into an upfront payment and one on delivery; maintenance and recurring automation are billed monthly.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the real scope, so we don't publish fixed rates. Tell us what you need over WhatsApp or the form and we send a closed quote, with no open-ended items.

Is there a lock-in on maintenance?

No. You can cancel whenever you want, with no penalty and no long notice period.

What if the scope changes mid-project?

We talk it through and adjust budget and timing before continuing — extra work is never added without you knowing first.

Do you support the project after delivery?

Yes, with the maintenance plans. Without one, we're still available for one-off issues billed separately.
